Privacy Policy

How we handle information connected with our website and aviation applications.

Effective: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Vectored Aero LLC (“Vectored Aero,” “we,” “our”) operates vectoredaero.com and develops Vector Airspace and Vector 214. This policy describes our website and these applications. The precise features available may vary by application version and your selections. It does not replace the separate privacy terms of Apple or other third-party services.

2. Information handled by our website

Our current website provides company and product information and an email contact link. If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and other information you choose to provide. GitHub Pages, the website host, can process technical information such as IP addresses and browser requests to deliver and protect the site. The website also requests Google Fonts, so your browser may communicate with Google to load those resources. We do not currently offer website account registration or a contact form.

3. Information handled by Vector Airspace

Vector Airspace provides aviation education and visualization features. If a version offers account, membership, or paid-access functions, identifiers and entitlement or transaction records needed to administer those functions may be processed. Apple handles App Store billing and in-app purchase transactions; we may receive purchase status and identifiers necessary to recognize entitlements or respond to support requests. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Apple. App settings and educational interaction state may be stored on your device.

4. Information handled by Vector 214

Vector 214 handles data you enter or generate, such as aircraft profiles, flight plans, navigation logs, performance inputs, and settings. Some data is stored locally on your device. When iCloud/CloudKit synchronization is used, eligible records may be stored or synchronized through Apple's CloudKit service. If you grant location permission and use related functions, the app processes location data to provide those functions. You control device-level location permissions in Apple Settings.

5. Optional OpenAI functionality

Vector 214 includes optional AI functionality. If you choose to use it, text, prompts, and user-selected documents you submit may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing. These files can contain personal or sensitive information if you include it. Do not submit material you lack authorization to share. An API credential supplied by an app owner may be stored using device Keychain facilities. OpenAI's processing, security, and retention policies are governed by its applicable terms and privacy documentation. These optional features are not required to use the main app.

6. Diagnostics, support, and other providers

Apple may provide developers with app analytics, diagnostics, crash information, or transaction reports, subject to Apple's systems and your settings. When you contact support, we use the information you provide to respond and troubleshoot. Service providers may include Apple (App Store, StoreKit, CloudKit), GitHub (hosting), Google (website fonts), and OpenAI (only when optional AI features are used). We do not sell personal information to advertisers or use cross-app advertising tracking as part of our stated launch approach. Any change to these practices will require updated disclosures.

7. Why information is used

Information is processed to deliver requested features, enable optional synchronization or AI processing, manage access or purchases, troubleshoot and support products, protect the services, meet legal obligations, and assess reliability. We seek to limit information handling to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

8. Choices, access, deletion, and retention

You can choose not to use optional AI features, manage app permissions in device settings, and control iCloud access through Apple. Deleting an app generally removes local app data but may not delete data held in iCloud, by Apple, or by external providers. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information that Vectored Aero controls by emailing info@vectoredaero.com. We may verify the request and retain limited records where needed for law, legitimate transactions, dispute resolution, or security. Retention periods vary based on the feature and provider; local records remain until you remove them, while support and business records are retained only as reasonably necessary for their purpose.

9. Security and international processing

We use available platform protections and reasonable measures appropriate to our services, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in jurisdictions outside your location under their own terms and applicable legal safeguards.

10. Children, rights, and updates

Our website is not directed specifically to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit their personal data through the website. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal information to us. Depending on applicable law, you may have privacy rights concerning access, correction, deletion, and other controls; email us to exercise rights over data we control. We may update this policy as our products evolve, with an updated effective date and additional notice when appropriate.
